The individual qualification round of the **Students National Cyber Challenge
2026**, run by **ISAA** on **Hack The Box**. A single 13-hour session, solo, 10
challenges across 7 categories, under a strict human-only policy (no AI
assistants, copilots or automated agents).

I finished **2nd of 209** with **10/10 flags**, 6,575 points and **3 first
bloods**. The top three all cleared every challenge on equal points, so
placement came down to time.

These are my walkthroughs for all 10, grouped by category. Flags are in the
`HTB{...}` format because the round ran on Hack The Box.
