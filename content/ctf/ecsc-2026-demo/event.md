An online demo jeopardy CTF for **ECSC 2026**, played with **Team Armenia** ahead of the
on-site event in Bochum. The challenges came from **CSCG 2025** (flags in the `CSCG{...}`
format) and covered reverse engineering, web, pwn, crypto, misc, forensics and game
hacking. We finished **3rd**.

These are walkthroughs of the challenges I solved myself - greyed-out ones are
challenges I didn't solve. I'll keep adding more as I work through the rest.
